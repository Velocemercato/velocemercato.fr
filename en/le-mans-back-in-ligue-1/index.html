How Le Mans returned to Ligue 1 after 16 years

Véloce Mercato · 22 November 2026

Two promotions in two seasons took Patrick Videira’s team back to France’s top flight. A visit to Marseille on matchday 11 shows how far the club has come.

Two seasons, two promotions

Le Mans reached Ligue 2 in 2025. One season later, a 2–0 final-day result against Bastia secured promotion again. The league’s disciplinary commission later ratified the score, confirming the club’s return to Ligue 1. For supporters, the wait had lasted 16 years.

What changes once you’re back

Promotion gets a team into the division. Staying there asks a different question. Can a club keep its identity while adapting to opponents that give it less time on the ball? Against Marseille, watch what Le Mans does after it wins possession. The first two passes can tell you whether it has a way out or is about to spend another spell defending.

The matchday details

The league scheduled Marseille–Le Mans for Sunday 22 November at 20:45 in France, or 19:45 in the UK, at the Vélodrome. Follow the action via the Véloce Mercato scores page.
